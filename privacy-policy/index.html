Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Blue Flame Construction, LLC, doing business as Blue Flame Glass ("we", "us"), collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you fill out a form, call, email or message us, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email address, city, the service you are asking about, and any details or photos you send about your project. Our website may also collect basic technical data such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location, through cookies and similar tools.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information to respond to your request, prepare estimates, schedule and complete work, send updates about your project, and improve our website and service.

3. How we collect it

Directly from you through forms, phone calls, text messages and email, and automatically through cookies and analytics tools when you use the site.

Optional website analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, Google Analytics measures website visits, traffic sources, approximate location, browser/device information, scrolling, page sections, button and link interactions, photo-gallery activity, FAQ use, chat opening and completed inspection requests. It uses cookies to distinguish visits. Phone and email clicks measure intent to contact us; they do not confirm a completed call or delivered email. We do not send form answers, names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact IDs or chat messages to Analytics.

The Google Analytics tag loads only after you allow optional analytics. Advertising storage, advertising user data and personalized advertising consent remain denied, and Google Signals is disabled. You can allow or decline analytics using Analytics choices at the bottom of each page. Declining stops this implementation from collecting Analytics and removes its Analytics cookies from this website; it does not delete data already collected. The form and chat remain available when analytics is declined and have their own operational storage.

4. Calls and text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. We do not sell your phone number or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our website and customer-management platform (GoHighLevel), our GoHighLevel/LeadConnector form that stores your request and notifies our team, and analytics or advertising providers such as Google or Meta if those tools are in use. If you choose to apply for financing, you do that directly with Acorn Finance under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email or call us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, state laws may give you additional rights. California residents: we do not sell personal information, and this site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal, tax and warranty record-keeping duties, then delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
